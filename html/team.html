Who we are

Built by students with a product mindset

GeoPS was born from a simple observation: thousands of small businesses in Lima offer incredible discounts, but their potential customers never hear about them. At the same time, millions of people walk past those businesses every day without knowing what they're missing.

We are PauLuDev, a team of five software engineers from Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas. We believe technology should democratize access to local commerce, not concentrate it in big chains.

Our vision is to become the reference platform for geolocated coupons in Latin America, transforming how local businesses manage their campaigns and how people discover savings along their daily route.
