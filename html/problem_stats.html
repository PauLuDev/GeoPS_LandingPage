The gap we close

Local advertising is broken

Thousands of small businesses in Lima offer incredible discounts every day — and their potential customers never hear about them. Meanwhile users suffer from ad fatigue: bombarded with irrelevant content, they ignore anything that doesn't respond to where they are right now.

GeoPS closes that gap by connecting in real time those who offer value with those who are meters away from receiving it.
