Built different from the ground up

Every decision in GeoPS was made with one question in mind: what does a local business or a nearby customer actually need?

01

Real-time, always

Live stock and active offers update instantly on the map. What you see reflects exactly what is available at this moment — no stale data, no expired surprises.

02

Map-first

The map is not a feature. Every interaction in GeoPS is anchored to your physical location and what is reachable right now, on foot.

03

Built for small business

No enterprise contracts, no agencies, no commissions. You set up your campaign, control the stock and own the results from day one.

04

Zero friction

GeoPS is a PWA. No download, no app store, no install. Open any browser, enable location and you are in. Works on any device, instantly.

05

Full-funnel analytics

Not just views. You see reservations, redemptions, and peak hours — all actionable.

06

Privacy by design

GPS is accessed once per session, only to center the map. No background tracking, no location history stored, no data sold to anyone.
