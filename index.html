As simple as walking

Two flows designed from scratch: one for customers looking for nearby discounts, one for businesses that want to attract the people already close to their door.

01

Enable your location

GeoPS accesses your GPS once per session to center the map. No background tracking, no stored history — you stay in control.

02

Discover on the map

Interactive pins show nearby businesses with active coupons. Filter by food, café, health, shops, or services — only what you need right now.

03

Reserve and redeem

Claim the coupon with a tap. Show the screen when you arrive at the store. Stock is real and live — if it shows available, it is.

01

Register your business

Create your profile in minutes, pin your store on the real map of Lima via OpenStreetMap, and set the impact radius you want to cover.

02

Launch your campaign

Set discount, stock, duration, and radius. Your offer goes live on your customers' map immediately — no middlemen, no waiting days.

03

Measure in real time

Your dashboard shows the full funnel from map views to in-store redemptions, with an hourly activity chart.

GeoPS closes that gap by connecting in real time those who offer value with those who are meters away from receiving it.

Built different from the ground up

Every decision in GeoPS was made with one question in mind: what does a local business or a nearby customer actually need?

01

Real-time, always

Live stock and active offers update instantly on the map. What you see reflects exactly what is available at this moment — no stale data, no expired surprises.

02

Map-first

The map is not a feature. Every interaction in GeoPS is anchored to your physical location and what is reachable right now, on foot.

03

Built for small business

No enterprise contracts, no agencies, no commissions. You set up your campaign, control the stock and own the results from day one.

04

Zero friction

GeoPS is a PWA. No download, no app store, no install. Open any browser, enable location and you are in. Works on any device, instantly.

05

Full-funnel analytics

Not just views. You see reservations, redemptions, and peak hours — all actionable.

06

Privacy by design

GPS is accessed once per session, only to center the map. No background tracking, no location history stored, no data sold to anyone.

Who we are

Built by students with a product mindset

GeoPS was born from a simple observation: thousands of small businesses in Lima offer incredible discounts, but their potential customers never hear about them. At the same time, millions of people walk past those businesses every day without knowing what they're missing.

We are PauLuDev, a team of five software engineers from Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas. We believe technology should democratize access to local commerce, not concentrate it in big chains.

Our vision is to become the reference platform for geolocated coupons in Latin America, transforming how local businesses manage their campaigns and how people discover savings along their daily route.
